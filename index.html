01 / 10 — The problem

Most studios run on memory and good intentions. Neither scales past a few bookings a month.

→

Which spreadsheet has the real numbers? Three tabs say three different things about the same booking.

→

Did anyone tell the second shooter the date moved? A reschedule in one chat rarely reaches everyone it should.

→

Has the deposit actually landed, or is that just what everyone's assuming this week?

→

Where did that proposal PDF go? — and did the couple even open it before going quiet.

Every studio hits a size where a shared folder and a group chat stop being enough. The usual fix is more tools bolted together — a form here, a sheet there — which multiplies the places something can fall through, instead of removing them.

02 / 10 — How it flows

One booking, one thread, start to finish.

Enquiry lands

Staffed & scheduled

Delivered & paid

Final balance settled, the booking archives itself.

08 / 10 — Data & security

The architecture underneath, in plain terms.

Not a marketing gloss — this is what's actually running underneath, described accurately rather than dressed up.

TENANT ISOLATION

Every table enforces Postgres Row-Level Security — forced, not just enabled. A studio's own query cannot physically return another studio's rows, whether or not the application code remembers to filter.

DATA ACCESS PATH

The browser never holds a database credential. Every read and write passes through one server-side data-access layer — a single, auditable path into the database, not one per feature.

ENCRYPTION IN TRANSIT

Every request — the app, the API, the client portal — runs over TLS. A booking, a payment note, a client's phone number: none of it crosses the network in the clear.

PASSWORD STORAGE

Passwords are hashed inside Postgres itself with pgcrypto (bcrypt, work factor 12). The plaintext password exists nowhere except the instant someone types it.

SESSION REVOCATION

Sessions are signed JWTs, checked against a live revocation flag on every single request. Change a password or remove someone from the team, and every session they held dies immediately — not whenever it happens to expire.

RATE LIMITING

Login, signup and password-reset are rate-limited through Postgres, not in memory — a limit holds across every server instance, not just the one that happened to answer a given request.

DATA PORTABILITY

Every booking, payment and client record exports as a full CSV, any time, from Settings — not a support ticket, not a data-request form. It was always your data.

PAYMENTS

Shinzo never processes a payment or stores a card or bank number. What's logged is a record you typed — the money moves through whatever channel you already use.

09 / 10 — Before you ask

The honest answers to what you're already wondering.

What happens to the bookings I already have?

There's no migration day. A new enquiry starts in Shinzo from the moment you switch on; anything already underway gets entered whenever you next touch it. Most studios are fully on it inside two weeks without ever blocking out the calendar for a changeover.

Is my clients' data actually safe?

Every studio's bookings, clients and payment records are walled off from every other studio at the database level — not a setting that could be misconfigured, the database itself refuses the request. Nobody outside your team, including other studios on Shinzo, can reach your data.

I already run everything through WhatsApp and Excel — why change?

You don't have to stop using WhatsApp to talk to people. Shinzo isn't asking you to change how you communicate — it replaces the part where the real numbers and dates end up spread across three places that quietly stop agreeing with each other. WhatsApp stays the conversation; Shinzo becomes the one place that's actually right.

What if I want to leave later?

Export the whole studio's booking history, payments and client list as a CSV any time, straight from Settings — no support ticket, no waiting on a data request. It was always your data; Shinzo just keeps it organized while you're here.

Do I need to be technical to run this?

If you can use WhatsApp, you can run Shinzo day to day — it's built to be used standing at a venue on a phone, not from a desk with an IT person on call. Setting up your studio's own rates and packages once, at the start, takes about fifteen minutes.

What does it cost?

Plans are matched to a studio's size rather than one-size-fits-all, so it's worth a straight conversation rather than a number here that may not fit you. Ask on the call — you'll get an answer, not a runaround.

Do you process our payments, or take a cut?

No — and that's deliberate. Shinzo never sits between you and your client's money. Collect the deposit by bank transfer, UPI, cash, whatever you already use, then log it — Shinzo tracks the balance, it doesn't move the funds. No processing fee, no payout delay, no transaction data of yours passing through a system that isn't your own bank.
